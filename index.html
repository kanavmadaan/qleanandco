Services

Cleaning services for homes, rentals, stores and commercial spaces.

Whether you need weekly home cleaning, a deep clean before guests arrive, a move-out turnover, or help with a messy property, the services below make it easy to see where we can help.

Residential Cleaning

Routine or one-time cleaning for houses, condos and apartments. Perfect for busy families, working professionals and anyone who wants their home looking consistently fresh.

  • Bedrooms, bathrooms and kitchens
  • Dusting, surfaces and detail work
  • Flexible repeat or one-time visits

Deep Cleaning

For spaces that need more than a quick tidy-up. Ideal for first-time cleans, seasonal resets, neglected rooms and homes that need extra detail work.

  • High-touch zones and overlooked areas
  • Built for visible improvement
  • Perfect for first-time intensive cleans

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

Detailed cleaning for move-ins, move-outs and rental turnovers so the space feels ready for the next tenant, owner or showing.

  • Rental turnovers and vacancy refreshes
  • Closing-day cleaning support
  • Ideal for apartment and condo units

Commercial Cleaning

Reliable cleaning for offices, shared workspaces, clinics and other business spaces where cleanliness, presentation and consistency matter every day.

  • Offices and work areas
  • Restrooms, touchpoints and floors
  • Scheduled maintenance options

Retail / Store Cleaning

Keep your storefront, showroom and customer areas looking polished with cleaning that supports a better first impression every day.

  • Showroom and customer areas
  • Front-of-house presentation
  • After-hours cleaning scheduling

Special Cleanup Jobs

For post-tenant messes, post-renovation dust, neglected units or other tougher jobs that need extra time, effort and attention to detail.

  • Heavy-duty property refreshes
  • Messy house or unit support
  • Custom quote-based jobs
